Extra information

How this works

This estimator works by estimating the total amount of balance given away in a day using the host leaderboard and total amount of users with auto join. It then uses a concave curve to estimate the multiplier your gem rank is likely to give you.

Assumptions

To work, this tool relies on several assumptions due to the lack of knowledge we have concerning the way EXP handles pools, gem rank and giveaways:

  • Every user who has auto is assumed to have given away 1B, except for the 15 in host leaderboard.
  • The members in /giveaway daily are assumed to be the only members to have hosted more than 1B.
  • The tool assumes everything stays the same for the next 24 hours; it can't predict future giveaways.
  • The 1B giveaways in chat mostly come from losses in chests, so to count them, the tool uses the CHEST EXP's stats in the leaderboard you paste.
  • Every user is assumed to have level 100, since everyone suggests new members to auto at level 100.
  • The total number of users is assumed to stay the same for 24 hours.
  • The 0.1–5× limits aren't assumed; they were shared by staff. Everything in between is calculated based on assumptions.
  • Your gem rank is assumed to stay stable for the next 24 hours.
  • The tool doesn't count the private giveaway pool for hosts, and the mechanic which gives everyone a 1× multiplier during the first 1B. Those add way too much complexity for a simple tool, so they might be added in the future.

Disclaimer

This is an estimation tool, not an exact prediction. The actual income you'll get may vary significantly due to the huge amount of factors it depends on, and due to it being rng.
